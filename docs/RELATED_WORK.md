The contribution here is an operational combination to evaluate: human-led codebook
rounds; PDF identity-to-field provenance; separate source reporting and team coding;
personalized independent/assisted offline workbenches; returned-file reconciliation;
and dependency-aware re-verification. These components individually have precedents.

TALL and Agency show where the design originated and how its unit/rules change. The
packaged skill adds generic contracts and checks; its newer features must not be
attributed retroactively to historical case data. No “first”, superiority, measured
speed gain or autonomous scientific-validity claim follows from this comparison.
