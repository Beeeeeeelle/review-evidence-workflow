# Follow Belle through a review

**Six illustrated scenes about organizing the work while keeping scientific judgment with people.**

Imagine Belle and her team beginning a literature review. They have a question, some PDFs and research rules they are still developing. This is where the story starts.

These Belle Black Comet illustrations explain the process conceptually. For the actual interface and project evidence, see [TALL](../cases/tall.md) and [Agency](../cases/agency.md).

## 01 · A pile of papers is only the beginning

![Belle pulls a relevant paper from scattered sources and notes, guided by her team's question.](../../assets/review-evidence-story-belle黑色彗星/01-the-question.png)

Belle already has papers. She still needs to know which passage supports a judgment, who wrote a note, and which PDF version belongs to the intended study.

She puts the team's question on the desk and asks the agent to organize the available list, rules and materials. **The skill starts by making the next step and the remaining gaps visible.**

## 02 · People develop the rules

![Belle writes and revises the orange-edged codebook against a chosen sample; AI organizes supporting material nearby.](../../assets/review-evidence-story-belle黑色彗星/02-people-write-the-rules.png)

The team chooses a sample, reads together, compares interpretations and develops its codebook. An awkward case prompts another revision. Ten papers might be useful; another sample may suit the project better.

**People decide what counts as evidence, how to code it and when the rules should change.** AI can organize those rules into fields, expose missing definitions and apply the current version. The orange-edged codebook stays with the team throughout the story.

## 03 · Find the paper, then check it

![Belle compares the study list with a full text and separates matched sources from those still needing checks.](../../assets/review-evidence-story-belle黑色彗星/03-find-the-right-source.png)

The agent helps locate full texts and organize files; software checks file information. Belle checks identity and completeness. A downloaded PDF still needs to match the intended study.

Missing or uncertain sources stay in an actionable queue. **Coding needs an identifiable source, and source gaps need to remain visible.** The team handles access and ambiguous judgments when required.

[Full-text retrieval, checks and handoff](../../review-evidence-workflow/references/pdf-handoff.md)

## 04 · Choose how people will review

![Belle selects a review mode: people can verify an AI draft against the source or code independently in a blank form.](../../assets/review-evidence-story-belle黑色彗星/04-two-ways-to-review.png)

With calibrated rules, the team can ask AI to prepare values, reasons and source locations. Each reviewer opens a personal workbench, reads the full text, then accepts, revises or defers.

Or people can code first in independent packages containing the human codebook, sources and blank forms, with AI and peer suggestions omitted. **Mode, records, fields and instructions are configurable by round or reviewer.** The two routes are options, not a mandatory sequence for everyone.

[See the real interface and both modes](../../README.md#two-review-modesand-your-own-ui-settings)

## 05 · Separate returns, shared judgment

![Reviewer A and B returns reach Belle's desk; she compares them with evidence while unresolved issues remain on a separate ledge.](../../assets/review-evidence-story-belle黑色彗星/05-return-and-resolve.png)

Belle sends each person a package. Reviewers save their progress, export JSON and send it back. The agent checks sources, versions and coverage, then puts differing judgments beside their evidence.

Now the team can discuss the actual disagreement: a missed passage, an unclear rule or a defensible difference in interpretation. **People adjudicate; unresolved questions remain open.** The workbench prepares that discussion without manufacturing human approval.

[Try saving, exporting and restoring a return](../TRY_DEMOS.md)

## 06 · A result you can revisit and reuse

![Belle replaces a v1 rule tab with v2, raising a Recheck flag on a linked decision; TALL and Agency can reuse the process with their own rules.](../../assets/review-evidence-story-belle黑色彗星/06-a-traceable-next-round.png)

The team finishes with a record linking sources, rule versions and human judgments. A corrected PDF or revised codebook points registered dependencies back to the work that needs checking. Earlier decisions and reasons remain part of the history.

The next project can reuse source preparation, personal packages, returns and decision recording. **TALL and Agency share these mechanics while people define each project's question, fields and appraisal rules.** Undeclared dependencies still need human review.

## Why work this way?

AI and software help with preparation, retrieval, organization and return checks. People can focus more of their attention on defining rules, interpreting evidence and resolving disagreement. Suggestions have a verification path, open issues have a place, and changes point back to affected work.

That is the intended practical value. How much time or error it saves still requires comparative evaluation.

**Start with a request like this:**

> Use $review-evidence-workflow. Here are our study list, draft codebook and available PDFs. Identify source and rule gaps, then prepare personal packages for our chosen sample. Our team will choose this round's mode and decide any rule changes.
