# Belle Black Comet: illustration production

These six AI-generated conceptual illustrations explain the human-led review workflow. They are not UI screenshots, depictions of actual reviewer sessions, or empirical evidence about TALL or Agency. The existing case pages and validation report retain those distinctions.

## Narrative and meaning

| Scene | Core action | Boundary to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Belle connects a research question to scattered source material | A pile of papers is not itself a completed review |
| 02 | People develop and revise the codebook | No fixed pilot size; AI organizes material rather than authorizing rules |
| 03 | Retrieval is followed by source checks | Missing/uncertain sources stay queued; identity matching is not scientific inclusion |
| 04 | Choose assisted verification or independent coding | Alternative modes, configurable per round/reviewer; blank packages omit suggestions |
| 05 | Compare separate returns and adjudicate with evidence | People decide; unresolved work stays open |
| 06 | Revisit affected judgments and adapt the process | Only registered dependencies can be flagged; projects retain their own scientific rules |

Orange-edged notebooks and binders represent human-developed rules. Blue slips and a small paper-sorting aid represent preparation or comparison support. Belle performs each main action. In Scene 04, the two stations are alternative assignments, not an obligatory sequence; neither implies blinding paper authors.

The story describes intended practical value: less manual organization and more direct access to evidence during human review. It does not supply numerical speed, accuracy or scientific-validity claims.

## Review before publication

Each image is checked for its core meaning, label accuracy, human/AI division of work, consistent Belle identity, short English annotations and clear composition. The human rules and source-bound decisions remain the conceptual focus. Image-generation drafts are not reviewer judgments.

Selected-output review: all six scenes passed the concept, role-boundary and character-identity checks. Each uses one full Belle with a continuous swept black hair silhouette, tiny eyes and one four-wing clip. Extra reviewers in Scenes 02 and 04 are bodyless head markers. The two review modes are visually separate, and uncertainty remains open in Scenes 03 and 05. Scene 06 flags a linked decision for rechecking rather than transferring an old approval.

One local text repair was made in Scene 02: the loose revision tab initially repeated `v1`; it was corrected to `v2` while retaining the attached `v1` tab and the rest of the drawing. All six selected files are 1672 × 941 PNGs (approximately 16:9). The sparse scenes retain a few light pen/contact marks beneath objects; they are editorial illustrations, not a strict vector diagram system.

The six images, bilingual story pages and README gallery were checked for consistent sequence and working local links before publication.
