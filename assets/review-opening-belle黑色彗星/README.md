# Problem-first Belle illustrations

Three concept illustrations introduce friction in literature-review work: finding the right full text, returning from a code to source evidence, and reconciling different review returns. They illustrate situations, not empirical outcomes or claims about another product.

Generated with the built-in image tool and reviewed against the current Belle Black Comet canonical reference. The original full illustrations are retained as concept drafts. They are not the current animation compositions. The first review was too lenient about character proportions and disconnected lines; [QA.json](QA.json) records that correction rather than treating every retained image as approved.

The current film uses six transparent layers. `02-belle-v2.png` and `03-belle-notebook-v2.png` replace the original character layers; older files remain for comparison. The new evidence-checking pose puts the lens on the source mark. The comparison pose places both hands on a larger notebook, with feedback paths meeting its two outer margins.

The renderer independently times entrances, moves the two editorial feedback tokens from opposite sides, and draws connecting paths progressively. Paths use object-relative endpoints, smooth curves, distance-based reveal and antialiased strokes. Connections appear after their objects settle. This is layered 2D animation; Belle's anatomy is not regenerated frame by frame. The white background is supplied by the composition.
